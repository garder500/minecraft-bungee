Prologue

A world that was eaten

The Devourer

A world-eater. It cannot be destroyed, only put back to sleep. It left only dust and pebbles.

Eight Guardians

Tired keepers of the world's laws, sealed in their Sarcophagi. Not evil: each one still holds something together.

You

A survivor waking on a fragment. Grow it, reach the Hub, cross the eight Sky Gates, and face what sleeps beneath.

Chapter I

Your fragment

No stone, no water, one lone tree. Every block you will ever place starts with a pebble.

  1. 1PebblesPick them off the ground. Nine make a cobblestone.
  2. 2The FountainA cobblestone every second, and the heart of every generator.
  3. 3Generator ringsRing the Fountain in stone bricks, sandstone, glass or nether bricks: Stone, Dunes, Frost and Ember generators.
  4. 4CoresBronze, Ember, Void Lens and Starcore: each tier brings new ores, from Bronzite to Starcore.

Chapter II

The Hub and the Quai des Huit

The last anchored fragment

Survivors camped in the ruins and hung lanterns on what was left. Merchants, the Archivist, the Contract Hunter and the Challenger's Ring gather around the amethyst Memory Spire.

Eight gates, all dark

South of the Hub, the Quai des Huit holds eight Sky Gates, one for each Realm. Every Guardian you defeat lights the next gates, for you alone. Then step through the glass.

Chapter VII

The Astral Interiority

The inside of the Devourer opens once all eight Guardians fall. Cross the Sea of Devoured Ideas, climb the Nervous Tree, and resist the pull of the Throne.

Secret

Between two pulses

The villagers cannot name it. They say “Hell Gao” and look at the sky between two stars.
