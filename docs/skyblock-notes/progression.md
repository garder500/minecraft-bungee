# Progression: pebbles, Fountain, multi-block generators, quest plan

Decisions (user, 2026-10-31). Source plan page: 266 quests in 14 categories (6 main path, 8 parallel).

## Start of the game
- **Pebbles**: right-click the ground of your island (empty hand or a stick) gives a Stone Pebble. 600 ms cooldown,
  200 per day, and no more once the player has made a Fountain. 9 pebbles craft 1 cobblestone (ExactChoice, so plain
  flint never works).
- **Fountain**: crafted from 4 cobblestone + 4 logs (no bucket: a vanilla bucket needs iron, which only generators give).
  A placed Fountain makes one cobblestone per second in the block above it, marked as generator cobble, so the ore
  table and the Miners work on it. It is the start generator and the centre of every multi-block generator.
- **Spring Stone**: an infinite water source. Right-click it with an empty bucket to fill it; it never runs dry. It is
  also the water cell of the multi-block generators. Quest reward of the second category.
- The old water + lava generator keeps working.

## Multi-block generators
A Fountain with the right 3x3 ring around it becomes a generator. Plan at the Fountain's level, the Spring Stone on
the south side, the Fountain in the middle. When the ring is wrong, right-clicking the Fountain says which block is
missing and on which side. `/guide` shows the blueprints.
- Stone: ring of stone bricks. Cobble + ores (existing tables and tiers).
- Dunes: ring of sandstone. Sand, gravel, red sand.
- Frost: ring of glass. Ice, snow blocks, chance of Frostium.
- Ember: ring of nether bricks, magma block instead of the Spring Stone. Netherrack, quartz, chance of Sunstone.
Tiers I to IV (Bronze Core, Ember Core, Void Lens, Starcore) change the ore tables (stone generator and the old water + lava generator); the tier stays on the player. The Frost ring is glass, the Ember heat cell is crafted from netherrack and coal, more Spring Stones from stone bricks around a water bucket.

## Quests
Categories carry an unlock requirement. The main path is ordered; the rest is parallel. Quests use the existing types
plus `EVENT` (a keyed event fired by a feature). A test loads the real config and checks counts, rewards, requires.

## Admin reset
`/islandadmin resetall` rebuilds every island and wipes, for every known player (also those who left their island):
coins, bank, auctions and bazaar orders, quests, fairy souls, generator progress and skins, skills, minions, boss and
dungeon records, Skyblock inventory and bed. Kept on purpose: Wings, island settings, menu preferences.

## Implementation notes (what is in the code)
- `survival-plugin/src/main/resources/quests.yml` is generated by `scripts/gen_quests.py` (267 quests, 14 categories).
  `plan: true` makes it replace the whole older tutorial (menu.yml and quarry.yml quests are ignored); the daily pool
  of menu.yml stays. A server's existing `menu.yml` is not touched, so nothing to migrate there.
- `QuestPlanTest` is the gate: 250+ quests, nothing dropped, reward items registered, forge/ore/boss/command targets real.
- Quest type `EVENT` takes a key. Fired keys: `pebble`, `spring_filled`, `forge_placed`, `machine_placed[:id]`,
  `machine_mined`, `generator_built[:kind]`, `dungeon_clear`, `dungeon_floor:N`, `dungeon_clear_nodeath`,
  `dungeon_clear_party`, `quest_done`. Add the key to the test's list when you fire a new one.
- The quest menu shows the categories (main path first); `/quests categories`, `/quests category <id>`; `/guide generators`
  prints the four ring plans; right-clicking a Fountain says what is missing.
- Cables are redstone wire under a 3D display (16 shapes by connection, `machine/cable_<mask>`, N=1 E=2 S=4 W=8). Old
  lightning-rod cables migrate at the next machine tick. A cable whose floor goes away gives the cable back.
- Gear: five tiers (bronze, rime, ember, void, starcore) of helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots (`armor_tiers.py`,
  worn textures included; armor values come from the base material: chainmail, iron, diamond, netherite, netherite, and starcore adds +1 or +2 armor and +1 toughness per piece on top of netherite),
  ember/void/starcore swords and picks, all made in the Forge (the Forge menu now has pages). Wearing a full set fires
  `armor_set:<tier>`. Endless Bucket (reward of the tier III quest) and Generator Plan (reward of the market quest) exist.
- World bosses are parked (they do not work): no ancient_colossus/storm_roc, empty pool, `/worldboss` not registered.
  Wings quests became dungeon floor 6 and 7 quests. `resetall` also resets the Wings.
